Get the DramaKan App

Experience ultra-fast loading, fullscreen immersion, and instant access right from your home screen. No App Store required—install directly securely from your browser.

Zero Background Data
Native Fullscreen
100% Safe & Secure
1

Open Browser Menu

While visiting DramaKan in Google Chrome on your Android device, tap the 3-dot menu icon in the top right corner of the screen.

2

Select Install App

Scroll down the menu list and tap on "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".

3

Confirm & Launch

Tap "Install" on the pop-up prompt. The DramaKan app will now appear in your app drawer and home screen for instant access.

1

Tap the Share Button

Open DramaKan in the Safari Browser on your iPhone or iPad. Tap the Share icon (the square with an arrow pointing up) at the bottom of the screen.

2

Add to Home Screen

Scroll down the share menu options and tap the button that says "Add to Home Screen".

3

Confirm Addition

Tap "Add" in the top right corner. The native iOS DramaKan app icon will instantly appear on your home screen.

1

Look at the URL Bar

Open DramaKan on your PC/Mac using Chrome, Edge, or Brave. Look at the right side of your web address (URL) bar at the top.

2

Click the Install Icon

Click the Install icon (it looks like a computer monitor with a downward arrow) located inside the URL bar.

3

Launch Standalone App

Click "Install". DramaKan will automatically open in its own clean, standalone window without any browser tabs or search bars.
